How a $230B Market Still Runs on Email

The infrastructure gap Vaizen was built to close

🗂️
Discovery Is Broken

Rights holders with completed titles have no persistent, searchable layer to reach buyers. Availability lives in spreadsheets and PDFs sent to whoever you met at a market.

📧
Licensing Lives in Email Chains

Avails, territories, and rights windows are negotiated through back-and-forth emails. A buyer in Germany shouldn't need three calls to know if European SVOD rights are available.

🏛️
Markets Are Gatekeepers

Cannes, AFM, and MIPCOM are expensive, infrequent, and closed-network. The infrastructure connecting indie supply to global demand shouldn't require a hotel room in the South of France.

Everything the Market Needed. Nothing It Didn't.

A full licensing workflow — built and running

🎬
Full Title Submission

Video, metadata, artwork, documents, and licensing availability in one structured workflow. Structured data from the first click — not a form that ends in a PDF attachment.

🗺️
Multi-Territory Avails Engine

Worldwide, include-list, or exclude-list licensing across 50+ countries. AVOD, SVOD, TVOD, Pay TV, and Free TV supported. Territory configuration is granular and real-time.

🔍
Advanced Search

Genre, country of production, cast, director, festival pedigree, content advisory, and release window. Every search returns only titles with declared, available rights.

👥
Role-Based Access

Filmmaker, Producer, Sales Agent, Distributor, Catalog Owner, Buyer, and Film Festival roles — each with appropriate permissions and workflows tailored to how they actually work.

🔒
Verified Identity Layer

Every party is verified at registration. Rights holders know who is accessing their screeners. Buyers know they are dealing with legitimate, authenticated rights owners.

1
The Rights Holders
