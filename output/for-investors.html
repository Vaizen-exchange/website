Risk Comparison

Why brand investment has a fundamentally different risk profile

❌

Traditional VC Model

If business fails, money is gone - spent on inventory, payroll, rent that generated no return. Capital disappears with the business.

✅

Brand Investment Model

Money flows through established media and marketing channels who get paid for services rendered, regardless of business outcome. Channels receive payment whether brand succeeds or not.

🔍

Following the Money

Brand-building capital doesn't disappear - it flows to Google for ad spend, production companies for videos, influencers for content, and media outlets for placement. The money goes to established entities.

Track Brand Performance

True foresight metrics that predict brand success

📢

Share of Voice (SOV)

How much conversation and mental real estate the brand owns in its category. Measures breakthrough and awareness.

Key Insight: Tells you if the brand is getting noticed in its market
🎯

Share of Market (SOM)

The brand's actual current market position. Shows if attention is converting to market share.

Key Insight: Reveals if awareness is translating to actual business
📊

Excess Share of Voice (eSOV)

The delta between SOV and SOM. Research shows when Share of Voice exceeds Share of Market, it's a leading indicator of future market share growth.

Key Insight: Predicts growth - winning mindshare before market share

Two fundamentally different approaches to funding brand-building

Traditional VC
