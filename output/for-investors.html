How Acquisition Used to Work

And why it needed to change

❌
The Old Way

Sales agents. Market badges. Screener links in email chains. Weeks of back-and-forth to find out if rights are even available for your territory. Expensive, slow, and closed-network.

✅
The Vaizen Way

Structured marketplace with declared avails. Search by any attribute. Reach verified rights holders directly. Interest confirmed in minutes — not after three calls across time zones.

🌍
The Cinando Problem

Cinando and TVSL are dated, expensive, and built around closed networks. Vaizen is open, searchable, and designed for how streaming platforms actually acquire content today.
